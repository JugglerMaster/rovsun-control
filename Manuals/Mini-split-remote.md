## Handling The Remote Control
Inserting and Replacing Batteries
Your air conditioning unit may come with two batteries (depending on the model). Put the batteries in

the remote control before use.
Steps:
1. Slide the back cover from the remote control downward, exposing the battery compartment.
2. Insert the batteries, paying attention to match up the (+) and (-) ends of the batteries with the
  symbols inside the battery compartment.

3. Slide the battery cover back into place.

                   The image is for reference only; please refer to the actual product.

Battery Performance Tips
For optimal product performance:
• Use 2 LRO 3 AAA (1.5V) batteries.
• Do not use rechargeable batteries.
• Replace the old batteries with new ones of the same type when the display is no longer legible.
• Do not mix old and new batteries, or batteries of different brands.
• Do not leave batteries in the remote control if you don't plan on using the device for more than 2
 months.

Tips For Using Remote Control
Recommendations for locating and using the remote control holder (if present).
The remote control be kept in a wall-mounted holder.

Notes For Using The Remote Control
1. Direct the remote control toward the Air conditioner.
2. Check that there are no objects between the remote control and the Signal receptor in the indoor
  unit.
3. Never leave the remote control exposed to the rays of the sun.
4. Keep the remote control at a distance of at least 1m from the television or other electrical
  appliances.

• In COOLING mode while powered on, the adjustable
 temperature range becomes 26-31°C:
1) If the original set temperature of the air conditioner is
  below 26°C, pressing the ECO button will force it to
  adjust to 26°C.
2) If the original set temperature is between 26.31°C,
  pressing the ECO button will keep the air conditioner's
  temperature unchanged.
3) If the set temperature is adjusted to below 26°C again,

  pressing the ECO button will exit the ECO function.

• In HEATING mode, pressing the ECO button disables
 the auxiliary heat function (depending on the model), and
 the adjustable temperature range becomes 16-25°C :
1) If the original set temperature is above 25°C, it will
  forcefully adjust to 25°C.
2) If the original set temperature is between 16-25°C,

  pressing the ECO button keeps the air conditioner's
  temperature unchanged.
3) If the set temperature is adjusted to above 25°C again,

  pressing the ECO button will exit the ECO function.

NOTE:
The ECO function is available in COOLING and HEATING

## Battery Disposal
Battery Disposal
• Do not dispose of batteries as unsorted municipal waste. Refer to local laws for proper disposal of
 batteries.

• Batteries may have a chemical symbol at the bottom of the disposal icon. This chemical symbol
 means that the battery contains a heavy metal that exceeds a certain concentration. An example is
 Pb: Lead (>0.004%).
• Appliances and used batteries must be treated in a specialized facility for reuse,
 recycling, and recovery. By ensuring correct disposal, you will help avoid possible
 negative consequences for the environment and human health.
